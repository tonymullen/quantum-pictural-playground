## Accessibility

- **Colour accessibility options.** Today Z spiders are green, X spiders red and Hadamards yellow. A red–green
  colour-vision deficiency makes Z and X hard to tell apart. Possible options:
  - an alternative palette chosen to be safe for colour-blind viewers;
  - telling spiders apart by more than colour, such as a fill pattern or a Z/X letter;
  - a high-contrast theme.

  The textbook's figures should follow whatever the app does. *Picturing Quantum Software* has a black-and-white
  edition, `pqs-bw.pdf`, for the same reason.

## Possible extensions from *Picturing Quantum Software*

*Picturing Quantum Software* (PQS) is Kissinger and van de Wetering's ZX-calculus book: github.com/zxcalc/book,
v1.3.0, CC BY-NC 4.0. We checked it on 2026-11-20 and it conflicts with nothing in PQP.

The textbook will not cover PQS. Its features are candidates for the Playground only if one turns out to be a good
extension. Possible ones:
- ZX simplification, such as graph-like diagrams, local complementation and pivoting (PQS ch. 5);
- circuit extraction from ZX-diagrams (PQS §3.4, ch. 4);
- phase gadgets;
- H-boxes.

Rule labels in PQS's style, (sp), (cc), (π), (sc), were considered and set aside for now.
